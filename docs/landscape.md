# EndNote & Word Citation Workflows: Existing Tools and Cross-Library Handoff Positioning

A collaborator sends a Word draft with live EndNote citations. You have a different EndNote library and a folder of PDFs. Record `#60` in the draft points to an unrelated paper in your library, while the actual paper sits at `#1` and a spelling variant sits at `#2` without a DOI. The practical question is whether you can reconcile those identities, add missing PDF-backed citations with attachments, and hand back an editable Word + library pair without breaking existing citations or corrupting either library.

EndNote already supplies the essential foundation. Its Word add-in stores bibliographic data inside each formatted citation, the *Traveling Library*, and Clarivate explicitly documents that the same reference can have different record numbers across computers. A visually clean reference list—or even a plain file hash manifest—therefore cannot prove that a subsequent edit or attachment lookup will hit the right record. [Traveling Library](https://docs.endnote.com/docs/endnote/2025/v1/windows/en/content/09word/the_traveling_library.htm) · [Working on different computers](https://docs.endnote.com/docs/endnote/2025/windows/v1/content/09word/working_on_diffrntcomptrs.htm)

## What the integration adds (especially for collaborator handoffs)

1. **Cross-library record identity reconciliation (`rebind_cwyw`)**: Compares DOI, PMID, and bibliographic evidence (tolerant to journal abbreviations and `±1` yr) between embedded Word citations and the target library before touching anything. In the 60-record collision test (`#60` pointing to an unrelated bat-sonar paper), it rebound `#60 → #1`, reused `#2` despite a missing DOI and 1-word title typo, added only `#61` (Wilson) with `0` edits to existing records, and blocked writes whenever another EndNote library window was open.
2. **Evidence-backed placements with PDFs attached in the paired copy**: Reads local PDF pages offline, verifies quoted text on the exact physical page (leaving unsupported claims blank), and attaches added PDFs cleanly inside `.Data/PDF` (`2/2` vs `0/2` in paired comparisons) so the next collaborator can open every cited paper directly from EndNote.
3. **A matched, Word-verified editable pair**: Formats through the installed Word + EndNote CWYW add-in on isolated copies (`4 → 3 → 4` main citations and `3 → 2 → 3` bibliography updates verified in Word 16 + EndNote 21) and audits field codes, record IDs, and Traveling Library bindings before delivery.

> **Scope & next verification step**: Native Windows Word, EndNote Desktop with CWYW, and closed-library snapshots are required for live formatting. A companion pre-open handoff receipt prototype (`handoff.py`) has also been evaluated on constructed mismatch/attachment-swap traps, and multi-author comparative trials are planned before deciding whether to fold it into a future release.
